Our Heritage & Craft

A Story Born of Deep Passion and Authentic Spices

At Ikku Bhai's Restaurant, cooking is not merely an occupation—it is a cherished cultural tradition passed through generations of spice masters.

Every dish that graces your table begins at sunrise. We source whole aromatic seeds, unadulterated cold-pressed oils, and farm-fresh cuts. Our master chefs gently roast spices over charcoal and patiently simmer gravies in heavy brass vessels, preserving natural aromas and deep, layered flavors that linger on your palate.

Chef's Masterpiece

The Legendary Slow-Roasted Tandoori Raan

An heirloom recipe perfected by Ikku Bhai himself. A whole leg of tender lamb is twice-marinated over 36 hours in malt vinegar, dark spices, roasted cumin, and raw garlic. It is subsequently slow-steamed and then char-crusted in the roaring tandoor to achieve unparalleled tenderness that pulls effortlessly apart.

  • ✓ Marinated for 36 hours in 18 royal aromatics
  • ✓ Served with saffron sheermal, mint relish, and spiced gravy
  • ✓ Ideal for celebration gatherings & 2–3 guests sharing

The Ikku Bhai Difference

Why Dine With Us?

❖

We hold dining to a higher standard. From our spotless open kitchens to our attentive table hospitality, every detail is engineered for culinary delight.

100% Fresh Daily Sourcing

No pre-packaged bases or artificial colorants. Every vegetable, cut of meat, and spice blend is verified and prepped daily.

Traditional Slow-Dum Method

We respect time. We cook our signature curries and biryanis in thick clay pots sealed with dough over low charcoal heat.

Passionate Culinary Masters

Our culinary kitchen team possesses over two decades of expertise refining traditional Mughlai, tandoor, and Awadhi cooking.

Warm, Attentive Hospitality

You are our esteemed family. From personalized table recommendations to comfortable family seating, your comfort is prime.

Immaculate Hygiene

Strict cleanliness protocols, sanitization stations, and open view cooking zones that showcase our kitchen's impeccable tidiness.

Celebrations & Banquets

Spacious private dining enclosures ideal for family reunions, anniversaries, corporate luncheons, and celebratory feasts.
